The biggest AI opportunity right now isn't another app. it's using AI marketing skills in a boring local trade where nobody else can run an ad. How to actually start: 1. open google maps, search "pool service" or "hvac" in your city. 30+ companies with 100+ reviews = demand that already exists. 2. pick a trade you have zero interest in. boring is the point, it means less competition. 3. find the operator who's great at the work and bad at marketing. you bring customers, they do the job. 4. don't learn the trade or buy a truck. own the front end only: seo, ads, automation. 5. run one simple ad with a real job photo, not a designed one. optimize for the phone call, not clicks. You're not creating demand. You're carving off a slice that's already there. Subscribe for more on using AI to grow real local businesses.…...more
