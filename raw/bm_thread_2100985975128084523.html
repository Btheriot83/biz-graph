the best AI business i've seen this year doesn't touch the AI industry. it's pointing AI marketing skills at a boring local trade where nobody else can run an ad. how to actually start this weekend: 1. open google maps, search "pool service" or "hvac" in your city. 30+ companies with 100+ reviews = demand that already exists, no convincing needed. 2. pick a trade you have zero interest in. boring is the point, it means less competition. 3. find the operator who's great at the work and bad at marketing. you bring the customers, they do the job. you never touch a wrench. 4. don't learn the trade or buy a truck. own the front end only: seo, ads, automation. 5. run one simple ad with a real job photo, not a designed one. optimize for the phone call, not clicks. you're not creating demand. you're carving off a slice that's already there.
