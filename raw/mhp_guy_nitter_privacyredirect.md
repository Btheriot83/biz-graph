The BO framework: Everyone knows to start businesses that have: • High margins • Growing market • Low competition • Low startup costs But how do you know if it's WORTH the HEADACHE to start a certain business or not? I have a battle-tested framework I call BO - binary outcomes. It helps you identify business ideas that are much easier to run, so you can get more reviews, more referrals and not hate your life. Remember, just because a business idea could work doesn't mean you should do it! Which business is more straightforward: A) HVAC or B) House painting A) HVAC - "My house was hot, now it's cold. B) eCommerce - I ordered a widget and it arrived on time. Thank you, I'll tell my friends. See what I mean? There's no shortage of ideas out there. Pick one that most easily facilitates a clear, binary outcome. You want BO. 😊 I've started too many companies that made me hate my life. Learn from my mistakes.

Thank you [@ToddLlewellyn](https://nitter.privacyredirect.com/ToddLlewellyn "Todd Llewellyn") for pointing this licensing thing out.

In NC, contracting for whole-home water filtration/treatment installs requires a plumbing license (or Restricted Limited Plumbing Contractor for filtration systems specifically). Statute GS 87-21 explicitly includes these systems. Subcontracting the work while unlicensed is not compliant. Pure equipment sales with fully independent homeowner-plumber contracts can work around it, but the described model (subcontracting as the provider) does not. A GC license does not cover plumbing trades or substitute. Todd is correct on the licensing issue.
