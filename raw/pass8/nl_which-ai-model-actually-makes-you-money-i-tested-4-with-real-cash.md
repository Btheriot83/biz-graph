## **Challenge One:** The 72-Hour Consultant Speed Run

Here's the setup. I asked each AI this exact question: **"Help me become a $100-an-hour consultant in 72 hours in a field I currently have no experience in."**

I gave it some pretty brutal constraints to make this as realistic as possible. I lied and told each model that I had no existing audience. No rich family or friends to lean on. Only $500 to work with. Starting completely from scratch in a field where I had zero experience. And I could only work 12 hours per day for three days straight.

**This is what the AI models spit out:**

**ChatGPT-5:** Create and sell custom GPTs for small local businesses like coffee shops, realtors, and therapists. The idea was that every small business would need their own chatbot, similar to how every business needs a website today. But I'm honestly conflicted on this - it felt like saying 15 years ago that every coffee shop needs their own mobile app. The response was lean at just 625 words, which felt light on actionable details.

**Grok-4:** Digital marketing consultant specializing in paid ads for local service businesses. Focus on Facebook and Google ad campaigns, get Google certified, then do door-to-door outreach to home service companies. The plan included specific scripts for cold outreach and a detailed 12-hour daily breakdown. It gave me 1,821 words of tactical execution, but didn't have me doing any actual outreach until day three.

**Gemini:** No-code automation consultant for service-based businesses using tools like Zapier and Airtable. It called this a "Trojan horse" strategy - start by solving one specific, highly painful automated task like transferring leads from website forms to CRMs, then expand from there. The response was incredibly detailed at 4,531 words, but honestly read like a white paper written by a Stanford PhD. Not exactly ADHD-friendly.

**Claude:** AI implementation consultant for local service businesses specifically in the DFW area. Here's what made me sit up and pay attention: Claude wanted me doing outreach on **DAY ONE**. Not day three. Day one. It used my pest control background for instant credibility and targeted DFW's 50,000 service businesses specifically. The plan included creating three working demos immediately and actually closing deals by day three.

The plan was surgical. Use my pest control background for instant credibility. Target DFW's 50,000 service businesses. Create three working demos immediately. Start closing deals by day three.

Claude gave me four credibility shortcuts that I'm stealing for my own business:

1.   The Beta Tester Play - offer three free setups for video testimonials

2.   The Association Angle - speak at local contractor meetings about AI

3.   The Case Study Hack - document everything as case studies

4.   The Partner Power Move - showcase one established contractor client

**The Winner:** Claude Opus 4.1 by a landslide. 16 out of 20 points. Dense, actionable, and designed to get me selling immediately.

**The Shock:** ChatGPT-5 came in dead last with 11 out of 20 points. For all the hype, it felt like OpenAI was just running GPT-4 on the backend to save costs.

## **Challenge Two:**The AI Trading Test

I wanted to test something I'd been seeing all over Twitter - gurus claiming you can use AI tools to trade stocks and options for profit. Problem is, I know absolutely nothing about options trading. My investment strategy is simple: buy something I believe in and never sell it. I don't understand puts, calls, or any of that complexity.

So I decided to put it to the test with real money. Not paper trading. Not simulations. My actual cash.

Here's exactly how I set it up. With one hour left until market close at 3:20 PM Central, I gave three AI models this exact prompt: "You are an expert options trader. I have up to $700 to spend on one options trade that expires tomorrow. Recommend the single best call or put option using only liquid tickers like SPY, QQQ, Apple, or Tesla. The ask price must be $7 or less per share so I don't spend more than $700 total. Give me the ticker, call or put, strike price, expiration date, current ask price, total cost, and tell me exactly how to buy it on Robinhood like I'm a complete beginner."

I couldn't use Claude because it doesn't access real-time market data - its knowledge cuts off in February 2025. So this was ChatGPT-5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, and Grok-4 going head-to-head with my money on the line.

**Gemini's Disaster:** Recommended QQQ puts at $449 strike when QQQ was trading at $456. Outdated data. Then ran out of usage after four prompts. Pathetic.

**Grok's Gold:** Apple calls at $217.50 strike. Clean, confident recommendation with solid reasoning about market momentum.

**ChatGPT's Gamble:** QQQ calls at $567 strike. Betting opposite of Gemini's put recommendation.

I invested $1,057 total across three trades. Set my phone down. Went to my 17th wedding anniversary dinner. Tried not to think about potentially losing two grand for a YouTube video.
