## The Step-by-Step Money Machine:

**Step 1: Find a white-label course**

Companies create comprehensive training programs for every trade imaginable. We're talking complete curricula with videos, tests, certifications - everything you need to teach someone a skill from scratch.

HVAC repair. Medical billing and coding. Pharmacy technician. IT help desk. Pest control. Pool maintenance. Solar installation. The list goes on.

These course companies sell their programs to anyone who wants to start a school. Cost ranges from $300 to $800 per complete course package. You're essentially buying a business-in-a-box.

**Step 2: Buy the course and rebrand it**

You purchase the white-label course and slap your school name on it. That's it. You now legally own a trade school curriculum.

The course company has already done the heavy lifting - created all the content, structured the learning modules, designed the tests. You just put your branding on top and you're ready to teach.

Some of Hector's clients started with courses they knew nothing about. One guy bought an appliance repair course, took it himself for the first time after five years of selling it, and ended up fixing his own HVAC unit for 20 bucks.

**Step 3: Find qualified students**

Here's where it gets interesting. The WIOA grant (Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act) targets specific groups of people:

*   Anyone who's been laid off from their job

*   People currently on food stamps

*   Anyone impacted by the justice system

*   Workers displaced by trade or technology changes

*   Low-income individuals seeking better opportunities

These aren't hard-to-find people. They're everywhere. The challenge is _most don't even know these grants exist._

Hector found his students by going directly to the sources:

*   Churches and religious organizations

*   Women's shelters and recovery centers

*   Workforce development offices

*   Community nonprofits

*   Unemployment offices

**Step 4: Government pays you directly**

Check out the WIOA (Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act) grants in your area. Every state has different regions, and every region treats the grant independently. That means different payment amounts:

*   Central Texas: $7,500 per student maximum

*   Some areas pay as low as $5,000

*   Others go as high as $15,000

*   Payment depends on the trade and regional demand

The government cuts you a check for each student who completes your program.   
Not _enrolls_ - **completes**. So you have skin in the game to actually help people **succeed.**

**The trick is it must be a job title with the word “technician” in the name. If it’s a job title that they haven’t funded before you just have to be able to justify it.**

**The Real Numbers Breakdown:**

Buy medical billing course: $500   
Government pays you per graduate: $7,500   
Train 10 students per month = $70,000 profit   
Train 20 students per month = $140,000 profit

And you can run multiple courses simultaneously. HVAC, medical billing, pest control, solar technician. Each one generating $5,000 to $15,000 per completed student.

One of Hector's clients was charging students $2,000 out of pocket for nursing courses. Half couldn't pay her back. She switched to the government grant system, raised her price to $9,999 (because that's what the grant covered), and went from $25,000 a month to $150,000 a month.

Same courses. Same students. But the government pays.

Hard states: California, Florida, New York. Two-year wait times. Rough. But that also means _way_ fewer competitors.

**Option 2: Add to Existing Business**  
Already own a business that struggles to find good employees? Start your own training program. **Get paid by the government to train your future workforce. Keep the best. Refer the rest.**

### The Action Plan:

1.   **Pick your trade.** Look at what's in demand in your area. Check job boards. What has the most openings?

2.   **Find white-label courses.** Google "white label [your trade] training courses." Compare prices.

3.   **Research your state requirements.** Every state is different. Some take weeks. Some take years.

4.   **Start building your referral network.** Churches. Workforce development centers. Nonprofits. Anyone who works with people who need job training.

5.   **Set up your systems.** CRM. Sales scripts. Email campaigns. Most trade school owners are tradespeople, not business owners. That's your advantage.
