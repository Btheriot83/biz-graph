Title: Shipping container storage rental business

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/714-shipping-container-storage-rental-business

Markdown Content:
Buy 20 or 40-foot shipping containers on Facebook Marketplace for $2,000-$5,000 each and rent them out as portable storage units at $150/month whole or $75/month per quarter section. Lease land on a busy road via a lease-purchase agreement to de-risk the model. You can recoup your investment in 1-2.5 years with ongoing passive income after that.
