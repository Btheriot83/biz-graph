Title: Broken electronics buyback and recycling/refurbishment

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/519-broken-electronics-buyback-and-recycling-refurbishment

Markdown Content:
Buy broken iPhone screens from repair shops at $2-3 per unit, test them for dead pixels using cheap testing units from China, then ship the working LCD assemblies to refurbishment facilities in Shenzhen where they are recycled into remanufactured screens sold for $6-7 each. This was the foot-in-the-door strategy for LCD Cycle -- offering repair shops money for waste they were throwing away, which built trust before upselling them new parts.
