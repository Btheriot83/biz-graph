Title: Car washing at Tesla supercharger stations

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/2487-car-washing-at-tesla-supercharger-stations

Markdown Content:
Washing cars at Tesla Supercharger stations while owners wait 30-35 minutes to charge. Use a two-bucket no-rinse system (like Optimum No Rinse), charge $25 per wash. Minimal startup costs - just buckets, water tank in a truck, and cleaning supplies.
