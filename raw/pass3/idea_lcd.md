Title: LCD Screen Recycling Business

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/1244-lcd-screen-recycling-business

Markdown Content:
Buy and recycle broken iPhone LCD screens, then sell/ship them to China for refurbishment. Chris started LCD Cycle after selling Phone Restore and grew it from $2.1M in year one to $4.8M year two, $9.6M year three, reaching eight figures. The business paid him millions and was sold after seven or eight years.
