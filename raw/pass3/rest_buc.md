Title: Open a Restaurant Near Buc-ee's

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/1431-open-a-restaurant-near-buc-ees

Markdown Content:
Open a restaurant or food business near a new Buc-ee's location to capitalize on the massive foot traffic it generates. Buc-ee's locations have 50-150 gas pumps and Tesla supercharging stations that draw huge crowds who spend $200-$300 per visit. The presenter notes that half a dozen restaurants and other businesses quickly pop up around each new Buc-ee's location due to its multiplier effect on local economies.
