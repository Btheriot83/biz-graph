Title: Premium RV park for blue-collar workers

URL Source: https://ideakoerner.com/idea/685-premium-rv-park-for-blue-collar-workers

Markdown Content:
Build or acquire an RV park in areas with high concentrations of blue-collar workers (oil workers, construction crews) and differentiate from existing low-quality 'man camps' by offering more space between pads, amenities like playgrounds and pools, and a better living experience at similar or slightly higher prices. Advised to buy an existing park off-market rather than build from scratch since acquisition costs are typically lower than replacement costs. Can validate demand by running competing Facebook Marketplace listings before committing capital.
