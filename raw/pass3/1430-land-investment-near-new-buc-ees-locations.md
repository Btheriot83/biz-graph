# Land Investment Near New Buc-ee's Locations

Purchase land near announced or upcoming Buc-ee's locations before they open, then hold and profit from the appreciation. Entire economies pop up around new Buc-ee's stores including restaurants, hotels, and retail businesses, dramatically increasing surrounding land values. Areas that were previously just farmland become commercial hubs within a few years.
