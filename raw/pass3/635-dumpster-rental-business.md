# Dumpster rental business

Start a dumpster rental company targeting homeowners and small contractors. Purchase dumpsters (~$5,500 each) and a truck, then rent them at ~$350/week. Acquire customers through Google Ads ($200/day), Google Maps/GMB optimization, and local community marketing on Nextdoor, Facebook groups, and Craigslist. The caller launched in Salt Lake City with 11 dumpsters, a full-time driver, and an office manager, with 12 more dumpsters on order within 6 weeks of starting.
