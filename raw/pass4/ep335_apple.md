I went out to Frisco, Texas to meet **Angela Henson**, a 20-year-old who turned her parents’ front porch into **Little Bit Bakery**, a home bakery doing around **$2,000 in sales every Saturday morning**. Angela showed me exactly how she runs the business, from baking **sourdough, cinnamon rolls, cookies and bagels** to managing preorders and selling out to lines of customers in about an hour. We talked about how she started without a big audience, how one simple TikTok helped the business take off, and the steps someone could follow to start their own **micro bakery** from home. We also broke down pricing, costs, cottage food laws, social media and why selling directly to your local community can be such a powerful business model.
